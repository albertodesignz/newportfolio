## Editorial and image provenance

Its Chart.js library selection reflects the owner's project constraint; chart previews in this frontend are rendered as SVG.

CRU's eight visuals are original embedded images extracted unchanged from the owner-supplied CRU.pptx, covering legacy interfaces, design exploration and redesign mockups. Historical workload figures are labeled as 2022 operating context. AI's contribution and the 20% productivity and 10% speed-to-cash increases were supplied by the owner in conversation; the deck itself does not establish these AI results. No specific AI model or unprovided automated action is asserted. The 70% AMP POC-time improvement is also owner-reported. The Design System narrative is proposed portfolio content.
